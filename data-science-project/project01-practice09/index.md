## Project Showcase
**Project title**: US Community Environmental Safety & Public Health Analysis
**Research question**: How does community‑level housing insecurity relate to adult depression prevalence across US counties?


This heatmap shows the pairwise correlation between housing insecurity and adult depression prevalence. The analysis returns a correlation coefficient of 0.02, meaning there is nearly no linear correlation between these two county‑level variables. Southern US states show higher average adult depression rates.
